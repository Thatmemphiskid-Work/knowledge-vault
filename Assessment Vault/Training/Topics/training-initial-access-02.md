Apply vendor fixes, reduce exposure and isolate service identities.

## Initial Access workflow

Defensive analyst training and harmless validation, not an attack execution manual. Use owner-provisioned identities, inert files and approved lab endpoints. Never collect real credentials or private communications. No snapshot/reset dependency. Commands are reference text and are not executed by the app.

Assess entry boundaries, identity controls, exposed applications and supplier trust without delivering harmful content.

### Inspect an approved web entry point

```sh
# Replace with an owner-approved lab URL. A HEAD request is not a vulnerability test.
curl --head --connect-timeout 5 --max-time 10 https://lab.example/
```

Capture status, TLS validation and request time. Headers can be incomplete or generated by a proxy. Do not disable certificate validation to make a check pass.

### Verify

1. List permitted entry points, identities, partner connections and supplier/update paths. Obtain synthetic accounts and expected allow/deny decisions.
2. For exposed software, use the CVE guide to reconcile deployed versions and configuration with vendor conditions. Do not infer RCE from a web response.
3. Exercise one ordinary authorized request and one explicitly approved synthetic deny case; correlate edge, application and identity evidence. No phishing delivery, malicious document or rogue hardware insertion.

### Fix / prevent

1. Patch exposed components, require strong identity checks and restrict administrative access and vendor connections.
2. Enforce trusted update provenance, device controls and independently verified communications.

### Retest

1. Repeat the same permitted and denied entry checks and confirm normal users still work.
2. Verify patch/configuration evidence across replicas, alternate routes and partner access paths.

### Detection evidence

1. Join gateway, endpoint, mail and identity events by asset, principal and UTC time. A successful login is not automatically authorized business activity.

### Decision branches

- **If Evidence or authority is incomplete:** Mark not tested or a coverage gap; do not invent a result.
- **If Expected deny/control fails:** Record the exact boundary and escalate to its owner; do not expand access.
- **If Expected collection/alert/containment is missing:** Locate the failed milestone using Analyst Training outcome branches and repeat after repair.
