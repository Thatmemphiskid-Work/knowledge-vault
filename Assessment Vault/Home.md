## Coverage, honestly
All 1,739 CVEs have NVD descriptions/version conditions, access-prerequisite metrics, embedded book-derived procedures, platform inventory guidance and an offline product-correlation command. Eleven synthesized methods explain evidence, interpretation, fixes and retesting without requiring book reading. Three catalog CVEs also have individually vendor-reviewed defensive guides. GitHub links identify defensive source or upstream fixes where available; automatically selected NVD patch candidates are clearly labeled unreviewed.

These are not 1,739 validated vulnerability detectors, CVE-specific exploit tests or exploit implementations. Weakness-family procedures and inventory checks do not prove exploitation of a named CVE. NVD/vendor conflicts and backports need analyst judgment; no vulnerable target builds were tested. No bulk exploit programs or stealth/detection-bypass instructions are included.

All 11 PDFs (5,391 pages) were text-extracted and indexed. This is not a claim that every page was manually reviewed or every technique was validated. 92 pages have little extractable text; diagrams, screenshots and scanned content may need visual review/OCR. The source notes list these counts.
